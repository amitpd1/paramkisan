Questions

Good to know.

Is this natural? Is any colour added?

No added colour, flavour or preservatives. Turmeric and chilli are simply dried and ground. Jaggery is cane juice boiled down without sulphur or bleaching.

How do I order and pay?

All orders are placed on Amazon.in, so you can use UPI, cards, netbanking or Cash on Delivery where available, with Amazon’s returns policy.

What is the shelf life and how should I store it?

Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. Keep the pouch sealed when not in use to protect aroma and colour.

Do you deliver to my area?

If Amazon delivers to your pincode, so do we. Enter your pincode on the Amazon product page to check delivery options.
